# Tamil localization review

The Tamil interface copy and Tamil Nadu theme text are localization drafts and should be checked by a native Tamil speaker before release. In particular, please review:

- Banking and business terms such as mortgage, unmortgage, auction, assets, and colour group.
- Pass-and-play, trade response, jail-release, and bankruptcy instructions.
- Compact button and tile labels for natural phrasing and fit at phone width.
- Tamil Nadu place/industry spellings and the one-line Tamil business facts.
- Festival card wording for tone and cultural accuracy.
